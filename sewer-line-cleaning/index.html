Sewer backup in the house? What to do right now

Wastewater rising out of a tub, shower or floor drain is a sewer backup, and it is an emergency, so call anytime because the line is open 24/7. Sewer line cleaning means clearing the main drain that carries all household wastewater from the house toward the street, and doing it with enough reach to remove the blockage and not just open a small hole. Stop using water in the house right now, including toilets, the washer, the dishwasher and showers.

  1. 1Turn off the washer, dishwasher and any running faucet, and ask everyone to stop flushing.
  2. 2Move people and pets out of the wet area, and lift rugs and boxes off the floor.
  3. 3Keep power off near the water. Use the panel only if you can reach it dry.
  4. 4Take photos of the water and any damage for your insurer.
  5. 5Call, and tell the plumber which fixtures are backing up and how high the water is.

Is the blockage in your pipe or in the street main?

The city runs the sewers here. The City of Loma Linda owns and maintains the wastewater collection system, and the wastewater is treated through a joint powers agreement with San Bernardino. That matters because a blockage in a city main is the city's to handle, so it helps to know which pipe is stopped.

For problems on the street side, such as a public sewer main or a manhole, the city lists an after-hours water, sewer and street emergency number at (909) 356-3805. If neighbors have the same problem at the same time, that number is worth calling while you also call us. The city pages we reviewed do not state the private lateral rule, so ask Loma Linda Public Works or the plumber to check where public and private responsibility meet.

A quick test helps. If only your house is backing up and the neighbors are fine, the blockage is most likely on your lateral. If the whole block is slow, the main is the suspect.

What cleaning the main line involves

Main sewer cleaning starts with a cleanout, a capped pipe fitting that gives access to the line, often near the house or at the property edge. A plumber removes the cap carefully, because wastewater may be standing behind it. A powered cable machine then runs into the line with a cutting head matched to the blockage.

Paper, grease and soft roots are chewed up and pulled back or pushed downstream. The plumber runs water to confirm the flow, then watches whether it drains clean or slows again. If a root ball or heavy sludge remains, a second pass or a hydro jetting run clears the pipe wall.

If the house has no cleanout, the plumber may pull a toilet or open a vent to reach the line. Installing a cleanout during the visit is worth asking about, since it makes every later visit faster and cleaner.

Why mains back up

Most main line stoppages have an ordinary cause. Wipes that were labeled flushable, thick paper, grease washed down the kitchen sink, and tree roots reaching a joint are the leading suspects. Each builds slowly until one load of laundry finishes the job.

A belly, a low section of pipe that holds water, collects debris every time the line runs. The blockage then returns in the same place every few months. Roots are different. They push in at a joint and spread into a mat that catches whatever passes by.

Median home construction in Loma Linda dates to 1984, and clay or cast iron lines from before then are more prone to rough walls and root entry. If your main has stopped more than once, a camera run tells you whether the cause is a habit or a pipe.

Sewer drain and main sewer cleaning, in plain terms

People search for sewer drain cleaning and main sewer cleaning when they mean the same job. The goal is a clear path from the house to the street connection. The method is a cable or a jet, and the proof is that water drains without gurgling and a camera shows an open pipe.

A line that has been cleaned should drain a bathtub and a flushing toilet at the same time without any rise in the lowest fixture. Ask the plumber to run water for a few minutes as a test before leaving.

Night, weekend and holiday sewer backups

A main line does not choose its time. Backups often show up on the heaviest water days, such as Sunday laundry or a holiday dinner with a full house. After-hours calls are normal for this work, and same-day appointments are offered when available.

If a backup begins late at night, the plumber's first goal is to stop the overflow. Waiting until morning can mean more damage to floors and walls, so call as soon as you see wastewater rising. Planned work, such as a camera run to find the root cause, can follow in daylight.

For single fixtures, such as a slow sink or tub, drain cleaning is the closer match. People in San Bernardino and other nearby towns reach the same line for main backups.

What affects the price of sewer line cleaning

Because every main is different, a number over the phone without details would be a guess. These are the real drivers.

  • How far the blockage sits from the cleanout.
  • The size and condition of the pipe, and whether it is old iron, clay or plastic.
  • What is in the line: paper and grease are quick, heavy roots take more passes.
  • Whether the cleanout exists or has to be created.
  • A camera run before or after, which adds time but adds information.
  • Night, weekend and holiday timing.

Many homeowner policies cover sudden and accidental water damage and may not cover a gradual backup or a pipe worn out by age, and some policies treat sewer backup as an optional extra.
