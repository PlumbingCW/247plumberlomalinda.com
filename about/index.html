About Loma Linda 24/7 Plumbing

A 24/7 emergency plumbing line for Loma Linda and the towns around it, with a clear price before any work starts.

Call (909) 505-3225See all services

What this service is

Loma Linda 24/7 Plumbing is an emergency plumbing line for Loma Linda, California and the towns next to it. You call, a person answers at any hour, and the job goes to a licensed plumber. The plumbing work is done by licensed plumbers, and the business is licensed and insured.

We cover drains, sewer lines, leaks, burst pipes, water heaters, fixtures and planned installations. The service pages list each job, and the work that sits inside a larger visit, such as backflow testing or storm drain cleaning, is described on the page it belongs to.

What you can expect from us

The promises on this site are short and plain. The line is open 24/7. The plumbing is done by licensed plumbers. You hear a clear price before work starts, and you decide whether to go ahead. We serve Loma Linda and nearby towns, and we say so when a job is outside that area.

We do not publish arrival times, since they depend on the job, the hour and the road. Ask when you call, and you get a straight answer about what is possible that day.

How a call works

You describe what the water is doing and give the address. We ask whether anyone is in danger, whether power is near the water and whether you can reach the main shutoff valve. Then we pass the job to a licensed plumber and tell you the price before work starts. If the problem can wait, we say so, and a same-day appointment is offered when one is available.

There is no form on this site and no online booking. Every request starts with a phone call, because plumbing problems are easier to sort out by talking.

What we do not handle first

A gas leak is not a plumbing call first. If you smell gas, leave the building, then call SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 or 911 from outside. Call a plumber only after the gas utility says it is safe. Fires, injuries and anyone trapped by water also go to 911 before anything else.

Why a call line instead of a counter

Plumbing trouble rarely arrives at a convenient hour. A supply line lets go on a Sunday night, a main sewer line backs up before a holiday dinner, or a water heater quits on the coldest morning of the year. A phone line that answers all day and night means the first step, working out what is happening and what to shut off, happens right away instead of waiting for an office to open.

It also keeps the process honest and simple. The same call that describes the problem gets you the price, and the price is stated before work starts, so nothing about the cost is a surprise when the plumber arrives. If the job turns out to be larger than the call suggested, the price is discussed again before anyone continues.

What to have ready when you call

Have the street address and the nearest cross street if you can. Know where the main water shutoff valve is, and whether water is still running. Note whether the water is clean or dirty, since that points to different problems, and whether anything electrical is near it. A photo of the water heater label or the leak is useful, though not required.

If you cannot find the shutoff valve, say so. The first minutes of a call are often spent finding it, and closing the main can save floors, ceilings and belongings while the plumber is on the way. The contact page repeats this checklist, and the services list shows what each job covers.

How to check any plumber's license

You should verify a plumber's license before you hire, and that includes us. The Contractors State License Board runs a public lookup at cslb.ca.gov, called Check a License. Enter the license number, digits only with no letters, or search by business name.

The record shows whether the license is active and whether any complaint disclosure or disciplinary history exists. The license history page also lists workers compensation and liability insurance information, and licensed contractors must carry a contractor license bond. Ask the plumber for a license number, and check it before work begins.
