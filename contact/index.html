Hours and how to reach us

The phone line is open 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. A person answers and takes your address and the details of the problem. This site has no form and no chat, so the phone is the one way to start a job.

For questions that are not urgent, such as a question about a past page or the site itself, you can write to contact@247plumberlomalinda.com. Email is not the way to report an emergency, so if water is running, call.

What to have ready when you call

A few details help the call go faster and give the plumber a better picture before anyone leaves.

  1. 1Your street address and ZIP code, including the unit number if you rent.
  2. 2What the water or drain is doing, in your own words, and when it started.
  3. 3Whether the main shutoff valve is closed, and whether you could reach it.
  4. 4Whether any water is near outlets, appliances or the breaker panel.
  5. 5The age and type of the water heater, if it is involved, and whether it runs on gas or electricity.
  6. 6Photos of the damage, if you can take them safely, and your landlord or property manager contact if you rent.

Before you dial

If water is running, close the main shutoff valve before you pick up the phone. It usually sits near the street side of the house, by a hose bib or in the garage by the water heater. Turn it clockwise until it stops. If sewage is rising, stop using every drain and toilet.

Keep the phone with you near the problem, because the person on the line may ask you to look at the valve, the meter or the water heater while you talk.

Emergency or can it wait

Call right away if water is moving into the house, sewage is backing up, there is no hot water in cold weather, or a pipe has burst. Shut off the main valve first. Call 911 for fire, injury or a rising flood, and leave the building and call SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 or 911 if you smell gas.

A dripping faucet, a toilet that runs, a slow drain and planned work such as a new fixture can usually wait for a daytime visit. Say so when you call, and a same-day appointment is offered when one is available.
