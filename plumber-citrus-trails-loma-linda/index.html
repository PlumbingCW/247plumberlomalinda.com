Citrus Trails Plumbing in Loma Linda, CA

Citrus Trails is a newer planned community in Loma Linda. This page sorts what the city documents from what is unknown, then points you to the right plumbing help.

Call (909) 505-3225All service areas

What Citrus Trails is

Citrus Trails is a master-planned community in the northeast part of Loma Linda. The city reviewed it as the Citrus Trails Master Plan and Tentative Map 18990, and the plan allows up to 581 residential units across roughly 112 acres. The city also runs two parks that carry the name.

It sits inside the larger Special Planning Area D, which the city calls The Groves at Loma Linda. Citrus Trails and The Groves are neighbors within one planning area, so this page covers both and mentions The Groves where it matters.

Where it sits

The city environmental record places Citrus Trails south of Redlands Boulevard, west of California Street and north of Mission Road. Mission Elementary sits at California Street and Redlands Boulevard, and the VA ambulatory care center sits near Bryn Mawr Road and Redlands Boulevard. The project is described as a Loma Linda project, and addresses on the city park pages use Loma Linda ZIP 92354.

Citrus Trails North is at 26351 Park Avenue on about 6.8 acres. Citrus Trails South is at 26350 Mission Road on about 4 acres. Use those as reference points when you call.

ZIP codes and school names do not settle the city

Some new homes in The Groves carry the Redlands ZIP 92373 in their listings, and the area is served by Redlands Unified School District. Neither fact proves a street is outside Loma Linda, and neither proves it is inside. Street level city limits for the newest streets were not verified for this page.

If you are not sure which city your address belongs to, check the Loma Linda service areas page before you call, or give us the address and ask.

Newer homes still need plumbing help

Homes here are recent, so do not expect the age related trouble you would look for in a 1960s house. A newer home can still have a failed fixture, a leaking supply line, a clog or a water heater that stops working. When that happens the question is the same as anywhere else: what do you see, and where is it coming from?

A tank that leaks or stops heating belongs with water heater repair. A damp spot or a meter that moves with taps closed points toward leak detection. A repeated clog is a drain cleaning job. If you are adding a fixture, ask about plumbing installation first so the work matches your layout.

Before you call

Before you call about a problem in Citrus Trails

Call (909) 505-3225 when you are ready to describe the problem. Have these four things ready.

Find the shutoff

Locate the main valve at the house and turn it clockwise if water is running where it should not.

Note your street

Give your street name and the nearest cross street, such as Mission Road or California Street.

Photograph the problem

A photo of the leak, stain or fixture helps you explain it clearly.

Keep power away from water

If water is near outlets or the water heater, switch off power only from a dry spot.
