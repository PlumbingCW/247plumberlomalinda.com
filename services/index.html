Pick the service by how fast the problem is moving

Start with one question. Is water or sewage still moving into the house? If yes, the job is urgent, and the fastest path is to shut off the main valve and call the 24/7 line. Active leaks, sewer backups and a water heater that is leaking or has no hot water belong in this group. The most common urgent pages are drain cleaning, burst pipe repair and leak detection.

If the problem is stable, you have more room. A toilet that runs, a faucet that drips or a disposal that hums can usually be fixed the same day without a rush. Planned work such as repiping, a sewer camera inspection or a new tankless water heater is easier to price and schedule ahead, since nobody is mopping a floor.

When you are not sure which group your problem is in, call. The person on the line asks what the water is doing and tells you whether it can wait for a daytime appointment.

Jobs that live inside a larger page

Some work does not need its own page because it is part of a bigger repair. Backflow testing and grease trap cleaning are covered under commercial plumbing. Storm drain cleaning is part of drain cleaning, and cast iron pipe repair sits inside sewer line repair.

Hybrid and gas water heater repair is handled within water heater repair. If you cannot find a job by name on the cards below, open the closest page, read the matching section and call. The same licensed plumbers handle all of it, and the price is clear before work starts.

Which page to read first

Drain and sewer problems are the biggest group. A single slow sink points to drain cleaning, while a line that clogs again after snaking points to hydro jetting or sewer line cleaning.

For water that should not be moving, such as a spinning meter or a warm patch on the floor, read leak detection and slab leak repair. For the pipe between the meter and the house, see water service line repair.

Fixtures and installs are the quieter end of the list. Toilet repair, faucet repair and garbage disposal repair are everyday jobs, and plumbing installation covers new fixtures and lines. Whatever you pick, the price comes before the work, and if you are unsure which page fits, call (909) 505-3225 and describe the problem.
