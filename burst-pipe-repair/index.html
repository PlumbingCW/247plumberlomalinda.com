Pipe burst and water spraying? What to do in the first three moves

A burst pipe is an emergency from the first second, and the line is open 24/7 for it. Burst pipe repair means cutting out the split section and rejoining the line with new pipe and fittings. Shut the main valve, open a faucet at the lowest point to drain the line, and keep everyone away from wet outlets until the water stops.

  1. 1Turn the main shut-off valve clockwise until it stops. If it is stuck or buried, call right away and say so.
  2. 2Open the lowest faucet in the house, and any outdoor hose bib, to drain the pipes and ease the flow.
  3. 3Flip off the breakers for rooms with standing water only if you can reach the panel on a dry floor.

Move valuables and rugs away from the wet area and scoop standing water with a bucket or towels. Photograph the split pipe and the damage before cleanup, because your insurer will ask for photos.

A burst at 2 a.m. or on a holiday weekend gets the same answer as a weekday one. Same-day appointments are offered when the schedule allows, and the first call can happen while the water is still draining.

Why pipes split here when winters barely freeze

The nearest long-record NOAA station, in Redlands, shows only about 3 days a year with a low at or below 32 degrees in its 1991 to 2020 normals. Those days fall in January, February, November, and December. That is a reading of the normals, not a count of local bursts, but it suggests that frozen pipes are not the usual story here.

More often a pipe fails from corrosion, pressure, or age. The Census survey puts about 42.5 percent of Loma Linda homes as built before 1980, with a median build year of 1984, so many houses have pipe that has been wet for decades. Ask the plumber what material you have and whether this split is one of several waiting to happen.

A rare cold night can still crack an exposed line, such as a hose bib, an attic pipe, or a run along a garage wall. If frost is in the forecast, cover outdoor fittings. Otherwise, treat a split pipe as a sign of wear, and use the repair visit to look at the pipe around it.

What a cracked or broken pipe looks like

  • ✓Water sprays or streams from a wall, ceiling, or the ground with no fixture running.
  • ✓A bulge or a hairline split in an exposed copper, plastic, or galvanized pipe.
  • ✓A sudden drop in pressure at every tap in the house.
  • ✓Wet drywall, bubbling paint, or a stain that appeared overnight.
  • ✓A hissing sound from inside a wall, or a meter that moves with all fixtures off.
  • ✓Rust-colored or cloudy water, which can mean a corroded section is failing.
  • ✓Wet insulation or soil around a pipe in a crawlspace or attic.

Copper, galvanized, PEX, and drain pipes: how each one fails

Each pipe material breaks in its own way, and the way it fails tells the plumber what to look for next. The cause matters because a repair that ignores it tends to fail again a few feet away.

Copper pipe repair usually deals with pinholes or a split. Pinholes come from acidic or mineral-heavy water attacking the wall of the pipe over years. A split often follows a hard knock or a sudden pressure spike. Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, narrows over time, and gives way at the threaded joints.

PEX bends and tolerates movement, but it can fail at a fitting or after long sun exposure. Drain pipe repair is a different job. Cast iron, ABS, and clay drain pipes crack from shifting soil or age, and a damaged drain leaks only when water flows through it, which a sewer camera inspection can confirm.

The plumber chooses based on pipe condition as much as the split itself. A repair that holds a damaged pipe together is only worth doing if the pipe next to it can take the pressure. If the pipe on both sides is thin or corroded, a longer replacement is more honest than a quick patch.

A pinhole or slow weep versus a true burst

Not every failure sprays. A pinhole leak weeps, a connection drips under a sink, and a corroded elbow seeps for weeks before anyone notices. These are leaks, not bursts, but they are the same pipe at an earlier stage.

Treat a pinhole as a signal. The wall of the pipe has thinned at that spot, and thin pipe seldom stays confined to one location. A plumber can repair the leak and then check the nearby run, and when the leak location is unclear, leak detection tells you where to look before anyone opens a wall.

Water in the street or at the meter? Check which side it is on

A burst on the house side of the meter is yours to fix. If water is gushing in the street, bubbling up beside the curb, or flowing out of the meter box, the break may be on the city's side. For those street-side problems, the city runs an after-hours water, sewer, and street emergency line at (909) 356-3805.

If the break is between the meter and the house, that buried run belongs to the homeowner, and a water service line repair is the job. When you cannot tell which side the water is coming from, close the main valve at the house, check whether the water stops, and call with what you see. The plumber can help sort it out.

Bursts at night, on weekends, and on holidays

Pipes do not pick convenient hours. A split under the kitchen sink at midnight, a burst line behind a wall on Sunday morning, or a failed fitting on a holiday all go through the same line. The call is answered 24/7, and the first thing you hear is how to stop the water.

What shapes the price of a burst pipe repair

  • Where the pipe is: an open garage wall is quicker than a bathroom wall with tile.
  • Pipe material and size, which set the parts and the method.
  • The length of damaged pipe that must be replaced, not just the split itself.
  • Wall, ceiling, or floor opening and the patching afterward.
  • After-hours work at night, on a weekend, or on a holiday.

Many homeowner policies cover sudden and accidental water damage, such as a pipe that bursts without warning. The California Department of Insurance lists seepage, leaks over time, and wear as generally not covered. Call your insurer early, photograph the damage, and keep the pipe section if the plumber removes it.
