Terms of Service

These terms explain how to use this website and our call line, and what you can expect from the information and service we provide.

About these terms

These terms apply to the website 247plumberlomalinda.com and to calls made to our line, (909) 505-3225. By using the site or calling the line, you agree to them. Last updated 2026.

Information only

The pages on this site are general information about plumbing. They are not professional advice and do not describe your exact situation. A plumber who sees your home, your pipes and your fixtures in person is the right source for advice about a specific problem.

Facts about local rules, permits and utilities can change. Where a rule varies, we point you to the agency that decides it, and you should confirm with that agency before you rely on it.

Emergencies

If there is a fire, an injury, a flood that threatens people or a gas smell, call 911 first. For a gas smell, leave the building and call SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 or 911 from outside. Our line is for plumbing, and it is not a replacement for emergency services.

How the service works

When you call, we take your address and a description of the problem and pass the job to a licensed plumber. The work is performed by licensed plumbers. Same-day appointments are offered when available, and we do not promise a specific arrival time, since it depends on the job and the hour.

Price

The price is agreed with you before work starts. The plumber explains the job and the cost, and no work begins until you say yes. If the job changes once the work is open, the plumber explains the change and the new price, and you decide again.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. By continuing a call, you accept that recording as described in our privacy policy.

Landlords, tenants and property managers

If you rent, the person who approves a repair is often your landlord or property manager. Tell them about the problem in writing, and make sure the person who agrees to the price has the authority to do so. We deal with the person who calls and the address given.

Using the website

You may read and use this site for your own personal or business needs. Please do not copy it, scrape it or use it in a way that harms it or other visitors. Links to other sites, including maps, are provided for convenience, and we do not control their content.

Limits

To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for losses that come from relying on the general information on this site instead of getting advice about your own situation.

We may update these terms from time to time. The updated version is posted on this page with a new last updated year, and using the site after that means you accept the change.
