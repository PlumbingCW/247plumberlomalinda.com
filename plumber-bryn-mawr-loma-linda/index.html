Bryn Mawr Plumbing in Loma Linda, CA

Bryn Mawr began as a separate community and is now part of Loma Linda. This page sorts what is documented from what is not, then points you to the right plumbing help.

What Bryn Mawr is today

Bryn Mawr is a historic community inside Loma Linda, in San Bernardino County. San Bernardino County history says part of it joined the city at its 1970 incorporation and that the rest was annexed in 2008. Today you will see the name on a city park, an elementary school and several streets.

This page is about the Bryn Mawr area of Loma Linda. It is not an official district, and the city has not drawn a published boundary. If you are unsure whether your address is in the city, the Loma Linda service area page is the better starting point.

Streets and places people use to place Bryn Mawr

Bryn Mawr Veterans Memorial Park sits at the corner of Juanita Street and Mayberry Street on 1.4 acres, and the city runs it. Bryn Mawr Elementary is at 11680 Whittier Avenue. Leonard Bailey Park is at 11555 Whittier Avenue, and the school shares it with the city.

The city General Plan lists eleven older properties on First, Juanita and Mayberry Streets as remnants of the historic Hispanic community of Bryn Mawr. It calls them a candidate for a historic district, not a designated one. Mission Road runs close by on the south side of the area.

Give those streets when you call. A cross street such as Juanita and Mayberry tells a plumber more than a neighborhood name does.

Why home age on this page is a caution, not a diagnosis

Census survey data for the block groups around Bryn Mawr point to a median build year in the 1980s for one and the mid 1990s for the other. Only about one home in six predates 1980. Those are survey figures with wide margins, so treat them as a rough picture of the area.

That means you cannot assume old pipes just because the community is historic. A house on one of the eleven older properties is a different case from a home built in the 1980s down the block. Tell the plumber the year your house was built if you know it, and let the inspection decide.

Which plumbing help fits which problem

Most calls from this part of Loma Linda fall into a few groups. A moving water meter with every tap closed points toward leak detection. A slow or repeated clog is usually a job for drain cleaning. A tank that leaks or stops heating belongs with water heater repair. If sewage shows at several fixtures at once, ask about a camera look with sewer camera inspection.

The city once ran a joint city and county sewer project with grant funds for the Bryn Mawr area, according to a 2004 annexation resolution. That history is useful if you wonder where the public sewer ends. For anything past the property line, ask the city first.

Before you call

Before you call about a problem in Bryn Mawr

Call (909) 505-3225 when you are ready to describe the problem. Have these four things ready.

Find the shutoff

Locate the main valve at the house and turn it clockwise if water is running where it should not.

Note the cross street

Name the nearest cross street, such as Juanita or Mayberry, plus your house number.

Photograph the problem

A photo of the leak, stain or fixture helps you explain it clearly.

Keep power away from water

If water is near outlets or the water heater, switch off power only from a dry spot.
