Water Heater Replacement Cost in Loma Linda, CA: What Sets the Price

What a new water heater costs in Loma Linda depends on a handful of choices you can see in advance. This 2026 guide shows which ones move the price.

By the Loma Linda 24/7 Plumbing teamPublished 2026-10-23Updated 2026-10-23
Call (909) 505-3225

The short answer on water heater replacement cost

The cost of a water heater replacement is the sum of the unit, the labor to swap it, and the extras your house needs to meet code. Two homes in Loma Linda can get very different totals for the same tank size, because the extras differ. This guide uses no dollar figures on purpose. A number printed on a web page cannot know your venting, your shutoff valve or how tight your closet is.

What you can know ahead of time is the list of items that raise or lower the total. Tank or tankless, gas or electric, where the unit sits, and what the old one is connected to decide most of it. The rest of this page walks through each one.

What is included in a typical replacement visit

A standard swap has a predictable set of tasks. Ask any plumber whether each line below is part of the quote, because it is easy for one bid to leave out what another includes.

  1. 1Shut off the water and the fuel, then drain the old tank.
  2. 2Disconnect the old unit and remove it from the house.
  3. 3Set the new unit, level it, and strap it against earthquake movement.
  4. 4Connect water lines, the gas line or electric supply, and the vent where the unit is gas-fired.
  5. 5Fit or check the temperature and pressure relief valve and its discharge pipe.
  6. 6Fill the tank, purge air, light or power it, and test for leaks and safe operation.

Items that often sit outside a basic quote include a new expansion tank, a new shutoff valve, new venting, a gas line change, and wall or floor repair after a leak. Those are the line items to ask about by name.

The two with the largest swing are the tank-or-tankless choice and the venting. A tank-to-tank swap in the same spot is the simplest job. Moving the unit, changing fuel, or going tankless usually needs a plan, not just a trade.

Tank versus tankless, and what each does to the bill

A tank heater heats and stores water all day. A tankless unit heats water only when you open a tap. Tankless units tend to carry a higher purchase price, and they often need a larger gas supply, new vent materials and sometimes electrical work. Whether the extra spending pays off over the years depends on how much hot water your household uses and what your utility charges, which a plumber cannot know without seeing the house.

If you are weighing the two, read through the tankless water heater page first, then ask the plumber to put both options in writing so you compare the same scope. A water heater replacement of a failed tank is usually quicker to schedule, which helps when you are without hot water.

What local rules do to the price

Loma Linda sits in the South Coast Air Quality Management District. Rule 1121 covers gas water heaters under 75,000 Btu per hour, and it requires low-NOx models, which is why replacement gas tanks sold in this area are those models. A plumber should buy a unit that meets the rule, so that is one reason a bargain tank on a shelf online may not be the right tank for you.

The district board voted down a proposal in June 2025 that would have pushed zero-NOx water heaters. No such rule was adopted, so you may still replace a gas unit with a compliant gas unit. Rules can change, and the district website is the place to confirm.

California law also requires water heaters to be braced against earthquake movement, so strapping is part of any proper install and part of the price. The state plumbing code in force from January 1, 2026 is the 2025 edition, and a plumber working under it will plan the relief valve discharge and the expansion tank to match.

Loma Linda local note: permits, water and gas

The Loma Linda Building and Safety Division issues plumbing permits and says plumbing work needs its own permit. The city pages do not name water heaters, so permit rules vary, and the best step is to ask the division at the counter number on its page whether your swap needs one. That one phone call can avoid an inspection problem when you sell the house.

Water hardness also touches the life of a tank. The city's 2025 water quality report lists hardness averaging about 78 parts per million, with a range from 7.8 to 200 across sources, and a second line that runs higher.

Because the city draws on several wells, hardness changes with where the water comes from, so test your tap or ask the city utility for its annual report. Harder water leaves more scale in a tank, which is a reason to plan a flush and anode check if you keep a tank.

For gas, SoCalGas runs rebate programs for high-efficiency gas storage and tankless heaters, funded first come, first served through the end of 2026, and its terms may change. Check the SoCalGas page before you buy, and do not assume a rebate is available. If you smell gas at any time, leave the building and call 1-800-427-2200 from outside, or 911.

What homeowners can do before the plumber comes

You can lower the stress and sometimes the bill by preparing.

  • Photograph the label on the old heater, including its size and fuel type.
  • Clear a path and a few feet of space around it.
  • Find the shutoff valve for the water and the gas, and note whether either is stuck.
  • Write down where any moisture or rust appears at the base.

Do not try to move, drain or disconnect a gas heater yourself. Gas, hot water and a heavy tank make a poor combination for a first attempt, and the licence rules apply: California requires a contractor licence once a job passes the state's cost threshold, or whenever a permit is needed. You can check any contractor on the state licence board's lookup page.

Where nearby homes differ

Closet size and venting differ from street to street. Residents in Redlands, about 4.2 miles east of Loma Linda, and in Grand Terrace, about 4.1 miles west, run into the same decisions, and we serve both. The tank swap steps above stay the same, while the extras change house by house.
